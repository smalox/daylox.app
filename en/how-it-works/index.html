How DayLox works

From sign-up to unlocking – here's a step-by-step guide to using DayLox.

Your path to a locker

1

Sign in

Download the DayLox app and sign in with your Google, Apple, or Microsoft account. No separate registration needed.

2

Choose a section

At large venues, lockers are divided into sections. Choose the section nearest to you.

3

Find a free locker

The app shows available lockers in your chosen section. Go to the displayed locker.

4

Lock via NFC

Hold your smartphone to the lock. The locker locks and your booking starts automatically.

5

Unlock via NFC

When you need your things, hold your phone to the lock again. The locker opens and the booking ends.

NFC Interaction

Just hold your phone to the lock – that's it. Locker management has never been this fast.

Understanding Sections

For large installations with hundreds or thousands of lockers, they are divided into sections. Each section corresponds to a physical area at the venue. This way, you can quickly find a free locker nearby without searching.

📡

Offline Mode

Your locker works even without an internet connection. All actions are stored locally and automatically synced when you're back online. Your access to the locker is guaranteed at all times.

🛟

On-Site Support

Phone dead? No problem. On-site staff can open your locker. Every support action is logged and you'll be notified by email – full transparency.
