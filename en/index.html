Your locker in 4 steps

1

Download App

Download the DayLox app and sign in with your Google, Apple, or Microsoft account.

2

Find a Locker

Choose a section and find an available locker near you.

3

Lock

Hold your phone to the lock – your booking starts automatically.

4

Unlock

When you're done, hold your phone to the lock again. The locker opens and the booking ends.

Everything in one app

Find, book, and manage lockers – all from your smartphone.

Offline-capable

Works offline too

No Wi-Fi? No problem. DayLox stores your actions locally and syncs automatically when you're back online. You always have access to your belongings.
